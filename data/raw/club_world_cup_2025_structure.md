# 2025 FIFA Club World Cup – Tournament Structure

## Group Stage (14–26 June 2025)
- **Format**: 8 groups (A–H) of 4 teams each. Single round‑robin; the top two clubs in each group advance to the knockout phase.

### Group A
- Palmeiras (Brazil)
- Porto (Portugal)
- Al Ahly (Egypt)
- Inter Miami (United States)

### Group B
- Paris Saint‑Germain (France)
- Atletico Madrid (Spain)
- Botafogo (Brazil)
- Seattle Sounders (United States)

### Group C
- Bayern Munich (Germany)
- Benfica (Portugal)
- Boca Juniors (Argentina)
- Auckland City (New Zealand)

### Group D
- Flamengo (Brazil)
- Chelsea (England)
- Esperance Sportive de Tunis (Tunisia)
- Los Angeles FC (United States)

### Group E
- River Plate (Argentina)

### Group F

### Group G

### Group H

## Knockout Stage (28 June – 13 July 2025)

### Round of 16
- Match 49: **Winner Group A** vs **Runner‑up Group B** — 28 Jun, Philadelphia
- Match 50: **Winner Group C** vs **Runner‑up Group D** — 28 Jun, Charlotte
- Match 51: **Winner Group B** vs **Runner‑up Group A** — 29 Jun, Atlanta
- Match 52: **Winner Group D** vs **Runner‑up Group C** — 29 Jun, Miami Gardens
- Match 53: **Winner Group E** vs **Runner‑up Group F** — 30 Jun, Charlotte
- Match 54: **Winner Group G** vs **Runner‑up Group H** — 30 Jun, Orlando
- Match 55: **Winner Group H** vs **Runner‑up Group G** — 1 Jul, Miami Gardens
- Match 56: **Winner Group F** vs **Runner‑up Group E** — 1 Jul, Atlanta

### Quarter‑finals
- Match 57: **Winner Match 53** vs **Winner Match 54** — 4 Jul, Orlando
- Match 58: **Winner Match 49** vs **Winner Match 50** — 4 Jul, Philadelphia
- Match 59: **Winner Match 51** vs **Winner Match 52** — 5 Jul, Atlanta
- Match 60: **Winner Match 55** vs **Winner Match 56** — 5 Jul, East Rutherford

### Semi‑finals
- Match 61: **Winner Match 57** vs **Winner Match 58** — 8 Jul, East Rutherford
- Match 62: **Winner Match 59** vs **Winner Match 60** — 9 Jul, East Rutherford

### Final
- Match 63: **Winner Match 61** vs **Winner Match 62** — 13 Jul, MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford

_Note: No third‑place playoff is held at this edition._
